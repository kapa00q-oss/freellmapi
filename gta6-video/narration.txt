35 quick facts about GTA 6 — one week before launch. Let's go!

Number 1. GTA 6 launches on November 19th, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.

Number 2. It unlocks at midnight local time, so players in the earliest time zones get in first.

Number 3. The standard edition costs seventy-nine ninety-nine in the US, and the Ultimate Edition is ninety-nine ninety-nine.

Number 4. Digital preloading opened on November 10th, more than a week early.

Number 5. Clear some space: the console download is about 212 gigabytes.

Number 6. Launch is digital only. A physical disc version is planned for 2027.

Number 7. There's no PC version at launch. PC players will have to wait.

Number 8. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams it from day one, even on the old Xbox One.

Number 9. The game was delayed twice: from fall 2025, to May 2026, and finally to November.

Number 10. Take-Two's boss Strauss Zelnick said he's highly confident November 19th is the final date.

Number 11. GTA 5 came out in 2013, making this the longest wait between main games in the series.

Number 12. GTA 5 has sold nearly 230 million copies. That's the bar GTA 6 has to beat.

Number 13. Rockstar first confirmed the game was in development in February 2022.

Number 14. That September, about 90 early development clips leaked online in one of gaming's biggest leaks ever.

Number 15. The game is set in Leonida, a fictional state based on Florida.

Number 16. Its main city is Vice City, Rockstar's take on Miami, last visited in 2006.

Number 17. Grassrivers is a swampy wilderness inspired by the Everglades.

Number 18. The Leonida Keys bring island beaches, boats and chaos by the sea.

Number 19. Mount Kalaga adds mountains and backcountry wilderness to explore.

Number 20. Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia round out the regions shown so far.

Number 21. You play as two leads: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

Number 22. Lucia is the first female lead of the modern GTA era.

Number 23. Their story is a Bonnie and Clyde style crime romance.

Number 24. The very first trailer opens with Lucia behind bars, in prison.

Number 25. The pair get pulled into a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the whole state.

Number 26. Other characters include Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Cal Hampton.

Number 27. Trailer one leaked, so Rockstar released it early, on December 5th, 2023.

Number 28. It got over 90 million YouTube views in 24 hours, a Guinness World Record for a game reveal.

Number 29. That beat MrBeast's record of 59 million views in a day for a non-music video.

Number 30. With 8.9 million likes, it also became the most liked game trailer on YouTube.

Number 31. The music was Tom Petty's 1989 song, Love Is a Long Road.

Number 32. Trailer two arrived on May 6th, 2025.

Number 33. Across all platforms it hit 475 million views in just 24 hours.

Number 34. Rockstar says trailer two was captured entirely in-game on a PlayStation 5.

Number 35. On PS5 you get adaptive triggers, police radio through the controller speaker, and 3D audio.
